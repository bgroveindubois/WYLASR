\# References and Related Work



This document records publications, projects, software, and other

technical work that has influenced the development of WYLASR.



The inclusion of a reference does not imply that the referenced work

supports or validates the experimental hypotheses being investigated by

WYLASR. References are included to document relevant prior work,

experimental methods, sensing architectures, signal-processing

techniques, and sources of inspiration.





\## ESPARGOS



\*\*ESPARGOS — Digital Phased Arrays, Made Affordable\*\*



Project website:

https://espargos.net/



Documentation:

https://espargos.net/docs/combined-arrays.html



Software:

https://github.com/ESPARGOS/pyespargos



ESPARGOS is a low-cost phase-synchronous Wi-Fi antenna-array and channel

sounding platform. Its architecture and associated software demonstrate

combined and distributed sensor arrays, calibration, synchronized

measurements, multidimensional data acquisition, and spatial array

processing.



ESPARGOS was a major early inspiration for WYLASR. In particular, its

combined-array architecture demonstrated a practical approach for

building larger sensing systems from multiple spatially organized

elements while preserving the geometry and calibration required for

computational analysis.



WYLASR investigates whether some of these general array-measurement and

data-processing concepts can be applied to a substantially different

class of experimental electromagnetic sensor based on planar bifilar

coil structures.



No ESPARGOS hardware or sensing mechanism is reproduced by WYLASR, and

the ESPARGOS results should not be interpreted as experimental evidence

for the sensing behavior being investigated by WYLASR.
